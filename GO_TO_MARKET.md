# StorePulse: go-to-market (all free)

## Landing copy
**Headline:** Your Shopify revenue moved. Here's why.
**Sub:** Upload your orders export. Get a plain-English explanation in 10 seconds: which products, orders or discounts caused the change. Your file is never stored.
**Bullets:** week-over-week breakdown | product-level drivers | discount and refund checks | works with a CSV, no app install
**Price (test it):** Free for 1 report/week. $12/month for unlimited reports and a saved weekly email (not built yet).
**CTA:** Try the demo / Get my report

## Validation message (Reddit r/shopify, X, Facebook groups, friends)
> I built a small tool that explains WHY a Shopify store's revenue went up or down week over week
> (which product, order count vs order value, discounts ending, refunds). I want to test it on 5 real
> stores for free. Send me your orders export (Orders > Export > CSV) and I'll send back the report within
> a day. You can delete or anonymise customer columns first: I only need dates, order names, line items, prices, discount codes.
> Honest feedback wanted: useful or not?

Rules: never post the same message in a group twice; read each group's self-promo rules first.

## Decision rule (after 2 weeks)
- 10 files received, 3+ say "useful", 2+ say they'd pay $10-15/month -> build Shopify connection + weekly email, then list.
- Otherwise stop. Cost so far: $0.

## What the buyer data can include
Ask for exports with customer emails/addresses removed. Never keep their files; delete after sending the report.
